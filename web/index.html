01 Design your sign

Fonts installed on the computer running OpenSCAD. Bold strokes work best.

Joined letters close gaps within this reach. Increase reach or reduce spacing until all desired letters connect; counters may also close.

02 Set the dimensions

Height measures visible lettering. Enclosure margin adds to the finished size.

03 Dial in the fit

Friction mode uses its own clearance instead of Face clearance. Print the fit samples and tune to your material; this is not a snap-lock.

INSET FACE ● SUPPORT LEDGE

Print the included fit sample before the full sign.

04 Route the wiring

X goes right, Y goes up when viewed from the front; origin is the artwork origin. Rear holes must land inside a cavity. The horizontal bore opens both sides and every wall it crosses, just above the back. It does not bridge gaps between letters. Inspect with the face hidden before printing.

Close contour follows the lettering; increase margin or reduce spacing if you need disconnected regions to join.

05 Mount the enclosure

Mounts are centered at ±half spacing from X=0. Place both inside solid back areas and clear of wiring. Keyhole slots extend upward; enter the head, then lower the sign. Select hardware for the actual load.

06 Hold the LED strip

Side lip caps the strip against the inner wall above the back. Back rails hold a straight strip along X, beneath two edge lips. Flex the strip into place; rigid waterproof sleeves may require more clearance. Measure the strip including components. Narrow cavities can truncate the rails; inspect before printing.

Before you print
  • Thin fonts and small details may leave too little room for LEDs.
  • Inspect wire holes and passages before printing; check mounting holes land in solid back material.
  • The face rests on a ledge; it is not a snap-lock.
